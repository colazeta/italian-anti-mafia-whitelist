# Licensing status

No licence grant is made by this draft repository yet.

The intended model is to separate:

- **software/code**: permissive open-source licence, to be selected before the first public release;
- **normalised datasets**: an open-data licence compatible with the rights and reuse conditions of the underlying public-sector information;
- **raw source documents**: retained with source-specific rights and provenance metadata rather than automatically relicensed.

A legal/reuse review is required before public bulk distribution, especially for fields relating to sole traders or other natural persons.
