## Quality and interpretation limitations

- Four exact listed-sector cells contain nonstandard source notation. Their row numbers and raw-cell hashes are pinned for review; only unambiguous, source-explicit section tokens are shown; raw source strings are retained, and invalid/fused tokens are never guessed.
- Two listed-source registration dates have malformed source typography and remain raw and unnormalised; formula-derived expiry dates are only used when the exact formula and cached date are verified. Two listed office cells are blank in the original.
- One applicant row has no company name in the official file; the public representation leaves it blank rather than generating an identity.
- The figures are **source-document occurrences**, not nationally deduplicated enterprises. Changes between snapshots are not evidence of entry, removal, refusal, revocation, interdiction or other changes of legal status. The site is a research archive; only official Prefecture sources have administrative effect.
- Address geocoding, ANNCSU, ISTAT/SITUAS/NUTS and spatial validation are explicitly **not release prerequisites**, following the owner's instruction. Source addresses remain unchanged.
- Official webpages may update again after capture and are sometimes inaccessible from third-party audit requests. The file identities are pinned and verified; the public link audit records access outcomes rather than silently inferring unavailable files.
